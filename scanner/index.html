Security Scanner

If you arrived here because you observed network traffic from one of our IP addresses, this page explains who we are, what we do, and how to opt out.

Who We Are

Huginn is a security intelligence platform that monitors the internet for exposed services, misconfigurations, and vulnerabilities. We operate a distributed sensor network that performs lightweight, non-intrusive scans to build a comprehensive view of internet-facing infrastructure.

What We Scan

Our scanners perform the following activities:

  • Port discovery — TCP SYN probes to identify open ports
  • Service identification — Standard HTTP/HTTPS connections to identify web services and TLS certificates
  • DNS enumeration — Public DNS record lookups

We do not perform:

  • Vulnerability exploitation or credential brute-forcing
  • Denial-of-service or high-rate flooding
  • Application-layer attacks or data exfiltration
  • Scanning of opted-out IP ranges

Active Scanners

No active sensors at this time.

Opt Out

We respect your right to opt out.

If you do not wish your IP addresses or networks to be scanned, send an email to abuse@meethuginn.com with:

  • The IP address(es) or CIDR range(s) you want excluded
  • Your name and organization

We process opt-out requests within 24 hours. Your IP range will be excluded from all future scans.

Contact

For questions, concerns, or abuse reports:

  • Email: abuse@meethuginn.com
  • General inquiries: info@meethuginn.com

Legal Basis

Our scanning activities consist of standard network probes that do not access, modify, or exfiltrate data from target systems. These activities are equivalent to connecting to a publicly accessible service with a web browser.

We comply with applicable laws and promptly honour all opt-out requests. If you believe our scanning has caused an issue, please contact us immediately at abuse@meethuginn.com.
